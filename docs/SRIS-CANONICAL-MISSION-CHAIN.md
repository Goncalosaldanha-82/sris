# SRIS canonical mission chain

This is the authoritative product, interface and reporting sequence for the
Pilot V1:

> Context → Observation → Evidence → Hypothesis → Alternatives → Decision →
> Action → Measurement → Outcome → Learning → Memory

Assumptions, constraints, gaps, uncertainty, provenance and confidence are
transversal epistemic conditions. They qualify the chain; they are not stages
that may be silently promoted into facts.

AI may draft and relate content across the complete chain. Every AI-generated
object remains a proposal until the applicable human review. Formal decision,
authorization to execute, validation of observed outcomes, publication of
learning and mission closure remain exclusively human acts.

The public capability endpoint exposes this exact sequence through
`canonical_mission_chain` and `transversal_epistemic_conditions`. Product copy,
operational reports and institutional material must use the same order.
